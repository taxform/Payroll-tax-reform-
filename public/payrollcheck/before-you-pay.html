Pre-payroll review

Answer from your own records. Do not enter employee names, wages, Social Security numbers, bank details, or other sensitive data. Answers stay in this browser session and are not uploaded by this page.

Review summary

Not a payroll approval. Any unanswered or flagged item should be investigated using your actual records and provider instructions before payroll is finalized. Even all “Reviewed” answers do not establish compliance.
