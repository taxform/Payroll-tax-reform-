What PayrollCheck 360 checks

PayrollCheck 360 is an independent review of your payroll software’s own records. It checks your 941s and 940, W-2s and W-3, and your deposit dates against the filing rules, and tells you what’s wrong before the IRS does. It is not proof from IRS records.

One flat $299 a year, filing fees included.
