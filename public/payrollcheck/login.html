Your privacy

PayrollCheck 360° asks for your email and password to sign you in, and a code from your authenticator app to confirm it is you. We keep a session cookie so you stay signed in (it expires after 12 hours, or 30 minutes without activity), and we record your sign-in attempts and the reports you view so we can keep the account safe and show you what happened. We keep sign-in records for up to a year and session records for no more than 30 days after they end. We never sell your information. Your payroll records stay yours — we check them against the filing rules, we do not share them. Questions or a deletion request: support@payrolltaxreform.com.
