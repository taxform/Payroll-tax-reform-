How Employers Should Prepare for Rate Changes

1. Update Payroll Systems

Verify your payroll software has updated its tax tables to reflect the new Social Security wage base, state SUI tax rate notices, and local withholding changes.

2. Collect Updated Forms W-4

Remind employees to submit a revised Form W-4 if their personal or financial situation changed, ensuring withholdings match their expected tax liability.

3. Review State SUI Notices

Every year, state unemployment departments mail an annual experience rate notice. Make sure this updated rate is entered into your payroll processor immediately.
