Year-End Payroll Checklist 2026

Get your payroll records ready before W-2 season. Use this checklist with your payroll provider or accountant to organize the review and record who will handle each step.

Start the checklist
When are 2026 W-2 forms due to the SSA?

The IRS lists February 1, 2027 as the deadline to file 2026 Forms W-2 and W-3 with the Social Security Administration, for both paper and electronic filing. Confirm employee-delivery requirements and any applicable relief in the 2026 IRS W-2/W-3 instructions.

Six steps to prepare for payroll year-end

Check each item during your review. Checkmarks apply to this page only and are not saved. Keep sensitive employee information in your secure payroll system.

Review employee names, Social Security numbers, mailing addresses, and your business EIN against your payroll records. Resolve discrepancies through a secure process before producing forms. Follow the IRS instructions for identification and corrections.

Compare annual payroll totals and draft Forms W-2/W-3 with your quarterly Forms 941, or the annual employment return that applies to your business. Investigate differences in wages and withholding and document valid adjustments. Use the reconciliation section of the W-2/W-3 instructions.

Ask the person responsible for payroll to review bonuses, reported tips, taxable fringe benefits, and any corrections before the last payroll is finalized. Use IRS Publication 15 and the applicable benefits guidance to determine treatment; different payments may need different handling.

Agree who will submit wage reports, deliver employee copies, and retain acceptance confirmations. Verify your electronic-filing requirement: the IRS generally requires e-filing when you file 10 or more covered information returns, counted across almost all return types. Review the IRS reporting guidance for exceptions and details.

Filing a return and making a payroll tax deposit are separate tasks. Confirm your deposit schedule for the next calendar year and review any special deposit rules with your provider. The IRS deposit guide explains monthly and semiweekly schedules. Use our state agency directory to check state requirements separately.

Retain payroll reports, filed returns, deposit acknowledgments, and the explanations behind reconciliations. The IRS generally requires employment tax records to be kept for at least four years; check Publication 15 and any longer requirements that apply.

Build a simple handoff sheet

For each task, record an owner, internal target date, open question, and confirmation location. Ask your provider when it needs corrections; its processing cutoff may be earlier than the government deadline. Review unresolved items together before approving forms.
