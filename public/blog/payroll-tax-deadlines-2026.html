2026 Payroll Tax Deadlines: The Employer Calendar

Late deposits are the most common — and most avoidable — payroll penalty. Bookmark this: every federal deposit rule and filing date for 2026.

Dates shift slightly when a deadline falls on a weekend or federal holiday — the next business day applies. If every deposit for the period was made on time, Form 941 filers get an automatic 10-day extension.

Deposits: monthly vs semi-weekly

Your deposit schedule comes from your lookback period (total employment taxes reported July–June). Under $50,000 means monthly deposits due by the 15th of the following month; over $50,000 means semi-weekly (Wednesday/Friday rule). New employers start monthly. All deposits go through EFTPS. Miss the schedule and the Failure to Deposit penalty runs 2% to 15% depending on lateness — which is why payroll software with automatic deposits pays for itself.
