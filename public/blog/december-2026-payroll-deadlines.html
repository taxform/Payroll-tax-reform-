December 2026 Payroll Deadlines: What Employers Must Deposit and File

December has one hard federal deposit date, a holiday trap for semiweekly depositors, and the year-end work that sets up the February 1, 2027 filings. Dates verified November 17, 2026 against IRS Publication 509, IRS Publication 15, and IRS Topic No. 757.

The short answer: Monthly depositors deposit November 2026 employment taxes by Tuesday, December 15, 2026, through EFTPS. Semiweekly depositors keep the Wednesday/Friday rule, but deposits due on Christmas Day (Friday, December 25) roll to Monday, December 28, and deposits due on New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, 2027) roll to Monday, January 4, 2027. December bonuses can trigger the $100,000 next-day deposit rule.

The one date every employer needs: December 15, 2026

If you are a monthly depositor — your total Form 941 tax liability during the lookback period (July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025) was $50,000 or less — you deposit the employment taxes on wages paid in November 2026 by Tuesday, December 15, 2026. That deposit covers federal income tax withheld plus both the employer and employee shares of Social Security (6.2% each, on wages up to the $184,500 wage base for 2026) and Medicare (1.45% each).

Schedule the payment in EFTPS by 8 p.m. Eastern on Monday, December 14 for it to count as timely. Calendar-year corporations also have a fourth-quarter estimated income tax payment due December 15 — a different obligation, but the same day, so don't confuse the two amounts.

Semiweekly depositors: the December calendar

If your lookback-period liability was more than $50,000, you are a semiweekly depositor for all of 2026. The rule never changes: wages paid Wednesday through Friday are deposited by the following Wednesday; wages paid Saturday through Tuesday are deposited by the following Friday. What changes in December is the holidays — a due date that lands on a Saturday, Sunday, or federal legal holiday moves to the next business day.

Watch the holiday weeks. Late-December paydays are where employers slip: a Christmas-week payday can push a deposit past its date without anyone noticing, and the failure-to-deposit penalty starts at 2% and rises to 15% depending on how late the deposit is. If you run a bonus payroll in December, check the $100,000 rule below before you run it.

The $100,000 next-day rule (December bonuses)

If you accumulate $100,000 or more in employment tax liability on any single day, you must deposit the entire amount by the close of the next business day — whatever your normal schedule is. A December bonus run is the most common trigger for a small employer. Hitting the threshold also converts you to a semiweekly depositor for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027. And if your total employment tax liability for the quarter is under $2,500, you can skip deposits entirely and pay the full amount when you file Form 941.

What December sets up: February 1, 2027

December is the last month of the tax year, and three filings land on the same day right after it:

  • Form 941 for Q4 2026 (October–December) — due Monday, February 1, 2027. January 31 falls on a Sunday, so the deadline rolls to the next business day.
  • W-2s for 2026 — due to employees and to the Social Security Administration on February 1, 2027. Reconcile the W-2 totals to your four quarterly 941s before you file; the IRS and SSA compare them.
  • Form 940 (FUTA) — the annual federal unemployment return, also due February 1, 2027.

Use December to confirm employee names, Social Security numbers, and addresses now — a wrong SSN on a W-2 is the error that costs the most time in January.

Frequently asked questions

When are December 2026 payroll taxes due?

Monthly depositors must deposit the employment taxes on wages paid in November 2026 by Tuesday, December 15, 2026, through EFTPS. That covers federal income tax withheld plus both shares of Social Security and Medicare. Schedule the payment by 8 p.m. Eastern on December 14 so it counts as timely.

What is the semiweekly deposit schedule in December 2026?

Semiweekly depositors pay by the following Wednesday for wages paid Wednesday through Friday, and by the following Friday for wages paid Saturday through Tuesday. In December 2026, that means deposits due December 9, 11, 16, 18, 23, 28, and 30, with the January 1 date rolling to January 4, 2027.

What happens if a payroll deposit due date falls on Christmas Day or New Year's Day?

If a deposit due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or federal legal holiday, the deposit is due the next business day. Christmas Day (Friday, December 25, 2026) and New Year's Day (Friday, January 1, 2027) are both federal holidays, so deposits due those days roll to Monday, December 28, 2026 and Monday, January 4, 2027.

What is the $100,000 next-day deposit rule, and why does December matter?

If you accumulate $100,000 or more in employment tax liability on any single day, you must deposit the whole amount by the close of the next business day, whatever your normal schedule is. December bonuses are the classic trigger, and hitting the threshold also makes you a semiweekly depositor for the rest of 2026 and all of 2027.

What payroll forms are due after December 2026?

The fourth-quarter Form 941, covering October through December 2026, is due Monday, February 1, 2027, because January 31 falls on a Sunday. W-2s for 2026 are due the same day — to employees and to the Social Security Administration. Form 940 (FUTA) is also due February 1, 2027.
