Frequently Asked Payroll Tax Questions

What are employer payroll taxes?

Employer payroll taxes generally include the employer share of Social Security and Medicare taxes, federal unemployment taxes when applicable, and relevant state or local employer taxes.

Do payroll tax requirements differ by state?

Yes. State income tax withholding, unemployment insurance, registration, reporting, and other payroll requirements vary by jurisdiction.

Can payroll software file taxes for employers?

Some payroll services offer payroll tax calculations, filings, and payments. Coverage and responsibilities depend on the provider and selected service plan.

Where can I verify federal payroll tax rules?

Consult official guidance from the Internal Revenue Service .
