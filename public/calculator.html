How Employer Payroll Taxes Work

Employers generally pay Social Security tax at 6.2% of covered wages up to the annual wage base.

Employers also generally pay Medicare tax at 1.45% of covered wages.

These employer taxes are separate from employee withholding and other employment taxes.

What's Not Included?

This calculator does not estimate federal unemployment tax, state unemployment insurance, state income tax withholding, local payroll taxes, or other employer obligations.
