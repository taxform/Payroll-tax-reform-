Evidence review

Document availability alone does not verify amounts, timeliness, filing acceptance, deposit posting, or compliance. Deposit schedules and deadlines depend on employer facts, tax liability, and applicable exceptions. State and local requirements, annual Forms 940/W-2/W-3, and special situations are not evaluated in this preview.

What a full verification requires

PayrollCheck must reconcile wage and tax amounts, filing acceptance, deposit obligations and payment posting against authoritative evidence, with a documented rules version and human review of exceptions. Secure uploads and payroll provider integrations will be enabled only after access controls, retention, encryption, and security testing are in place.
