Essential IRS Employer Tax Forms

Official IRS forms required for employee onboarding, quarterly withholding reporting, and annual tax reconciliation:

Employee Onboarding

IRS Form W-4

Employee's Withholding Certificate. Completed by new hires and updated when allowances change. Instructs the employer on how much federal income tax to withhold from each paycheck.

Download Form W-4 from IRS.gov →
Quarterly Filing

IRS Form 941

Employer's Quarterly Federal Tax Return. Reports federal income taxes withheld, employee Social Security and Medicare taxes, and employer matching shares.

Download Form 941 & Instructions →
Annual Unemployment

IRS Form 940

Employer's Annual Federal Unemployment (FUTA) Tax Return. Reconciles annual unemployment tax liability and validates timely state unemployment tax credits.

Download Form 940 & Schedule A →
Year-End Wages

Forms W-2 & W-3

Wage and Tax Statement (W-2) and Transmittal (W-3). Discloses gross wages, FICA deductions, and tax withholdings to employees and the Social Security Administration.

IRS Form W-2 Information →
Contractors

Form 1099-NEC & W-9

Nonemployee Compensation (1099-NEC) and Request for Taxpayer Identification Number (W-9). Used for reporting compensation of $600 or more to independent contractors.

Core Publication

Small Business Payroll Compliance Checklist

At Employee Hire

  • Obtain signed Form W-4 (Federal Withholding)
  • Collect state employee tax withholding certificate
  • Verify employment eligibility with Form I-9
  • Submit state new hire report within statutory deadline (usually 20 days)

Each Pay Period

  • Calculate gross wages, overtime, and pre-tax deductions
  • Withhold federal, state, and local income taxes
  • Withhold and match 6.2% Social Security and 1.45% Medicare (FICA)
  • Remit tax deposits via EFTPS according to assigned schedule

Quarterly Duties

  • File IRS Form 941 by April 30, July 31, October 31, January 31
  • File state quarterly wage and unemployment insurance (SUI) returns
  • Reconcile tax deposits against total quarter payroll reports

Annual Year-End Duties

  • Distribute Form W-2 to all employees by January 31
  • Transmit Copy A of W-2 and Form W-3 to SSA by January 31
  • File IRS Form 940 (Annual Federal Unemployment) by January 31
  • Distribute Forms 1099-NEC to independent contractors by January 31
